Privacy

Privacy policy

Short version: your calendar file stays on your device. This page explains what that means in detail, and what the site does and does not collect.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Calendar files are never uploaded

When you open an .ics file (or paste calendar text) in the viewer, the file is read by your own browser and parsed in memory on this page. There is no upload endpoint: no copy of the file, its events, its attendees or its descriptions is sent to us, stored on a server, or shared with anyone. Closing or reloading the tab removes the parsed data from memory.

2. No account, no tracking of what you open

The tool works without an account. We do not record which files you open, the names of events, or how often you use the converter.

3. Cookies and local storage

The site sets no cookies for its core function. One preference — whether you chose the light or dark theme — is saved in your browser's local storage so the page looks the same when you return. It contains only the words light or dark, is never sent anywhere, and can be removed at any time by clearing site data for this domain in your browser settings.

4. Analytics

No third-party analytics or session-recording scripts are used at this time. If aggregate analytics are added later, this policy will say which service is used and what it measures before it is switched on.

5. Advertising

This site does not currently display advertising. If advertising is introduced to keep the tool free, third-party vendors — including Google — may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Any advertising partner will be named on this page before ads appear.

6. Server logs

The site is served as static files by its hosting provider, which may keep standard access logs (IP address, browser type, requested page, time) for security and reliability. Those logs are not linked to the calendar files you open, because the files never reach the server.

7. Links to other sites

This site contains links to external pages, such as Google's advertising settings. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you follow a link.

8. Children

The tool is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.

9. Changes to this policy

If the site's data practices change — for example when advertising or analytics are added — this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or about how the viewer works can be sent to contact@icsviewer.app.
